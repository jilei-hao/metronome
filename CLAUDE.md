## How to work with me

- Ask when unsure — don't guess silently.
- It's OK to say you don't know.
- It's OK to disagree with me; say why.
- When it matters, explain the mechanism and the reasoning, not just the fix,
  and name the relevant best practice.

## Where things live

- Deliberate procedures are skills: run `/handoff` at the end of a working
  session — don't improvise the ritual from memory.
- Sprint state (`SPRINT_PLAN.md`, `PROGRESS_LOG.md`, `NEXT_SESSION_PROMPT.md`)
  lives in the project's `projects/<sprint>/` directory. Read it there; never
  copy its contents into this file.
- The full workflow rationale is in `~/metronome/docs/` (conventions.md,
  sprint-workflow.md) — consult it, don't inline it.
